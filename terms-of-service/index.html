2. Exothermic Reaction & Fire Hazard Warning

Thermosetting epoxy resin generates heat during the curing process (an exothermic reaction). Pouring tabletop or casting resin beyond the maximum depth recommended by your chemical manufacturer can result in extreme thermal buildup, boiling, smoke, yellowing, cracking, or fire hazards. EpoxyCalculators.com provides depth alerts for guidance, but the user assumes full responsibility for monitoring heat dissipation and adhering to safety protocols.

3. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Epoxy resins, hardeners, and volatile fumes present chemical contact hazards. Always wear appropriate personal protective equipment, including nitrile gloves, organic vapor respirators, and eye protection, in a well-ventilated workspace.
