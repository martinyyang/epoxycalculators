Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

At EpoxyCalculators.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent, responsible data practices. This Privacy Policy explains our client-side computing architecture, how we collect anonymous usage data, and our disclosures regarding third-party advertising cookies in full compliance with Google AdSense policies, CCPA, and GDPR.

1. 100% Client-Side Project Calculations

All project dimensions, mold measurements, river table stations (W1–W5), mixing ratios, and weight/volume conversions are processed 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not store, log, or transmit your workshop dimensions, material prices, or custom calculations to any remote server or database.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We partner with third-party vendors, including Google LLC, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and related tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to EpoxyCalculators.com or other sites on the Internet.

Google Advertising Cookie Notice:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to users based on their visit to EpoxyCalculators.com and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Web Analytics (Google Analytics & Microsoft Clarity)

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate visitor patterns, such as calculation modes used, device screen resolutions, and general geographic regions (e.g. United States). Microsoft Clarity captures anonymized interaction metrics (such as heatmaps and scroll depth) to help us identify layout bugs and improve the calculator interface. These analytics tools do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & GDPR

Under CCPA and GDPR regulations, users have the right to request information about data collection and opt out of third-party cookie tracking. Because we do not sell personal information or maintain user accounts, our data collection is limited exclusively to anonymous technical telemetry and standard advertising cookies described above.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to report a privacy concern, please contact our administrative team via email at support@epoxycalculators.com.
