# EpoxyCalculators.com — US Engineering Epoxy Resin Volume, Gram Scale & Coverage Suite

[![Live Site](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Tool-epoxycalculators.com-d97706?style=for-the-badge)](https://epoxycalculators.com/)

**EpoxyCalculators.com** is a fast, 100% client-side + static-prerendered engineering calculator suite built for US woodworkers, river table makers, bar top builders, and garage floor contractors.

## 🔗 Live Calculators

1. **[Universal Epoxy Resin Calculator (Volume + Part A/B Grams)](https://epoxycalculators.com/)**
   - Converts rectangular, circular, and irregular mold dimensions (`inches`) into exact **US Gallons**, **Fluid Ounces (`fl oz`)**, **Quarts**, **Milliliters (`mL`)**, and **Digital Scale Gram Weights (`g`)** for `1:1`, `2:1`, and `3:1` systems.
2. **[Deep Pour & River Table Epoxy Calculator (5-Point Live Edge Channel)](https://epoxycalculators.com/deep-pour-river-table-calculator/)**
   - Integrates 5 width stations (`W1–W5`) along irregular walnut/maple live-edge voids, applies bevel under-cut compensation (`+10%`), and triggers exothermic heat warnings when pour depth exceeds `2.0 inches`.
3. **[Table Top & Countertop Epoxy Coverage Calculator (Seal Coat + Flood Coat)](https://epoxycalculators.com/table-top-countertop-calculator/)**
   - Separates total batch volume into a `1/32"` (`0.03125"`) porous wood seal coat and a `1/8"` (`0.125"`) self-leveling flood coat, plus perimeter edge-drip waste.
4. **[Epoxy Garage Floor & Cost Calculator (Mils, Flake lbs & Decimal Budget)](https://epoxycalculators.com/garage-floor-cost-calculator/)**
   - Uses the US coatings industry constant (`1 Gallon = 1,604 sq.ft @ 1 mil` for 100% solids resin) to compute exact gallons, vinyl flake broadcast pounds (`lbs`), and cent-accurate DIY vs. professional installation budgets.
